# RECOGNISE — what the camera actually knows

A held-out measurement of the photo-enrol -> recognise loop: teach one photo of a packet, then ask the counter to name and price it from views it has never seen.

Generated by `tools/bench_recognise.py` in 11.25 s. Deterministic — every seed is fixed, so re-running reproduces these numbers exactly.

```
./.venv/bin/python tools/bench_recognise.py
```

## The three numbers

Accuracy alone is the easiest thing here to lie with: a recogniser buys it by abstaining on everything hard, and buys it again by never being asked about products it was not taught. So all three are reported together, over the same items, and the untaught packets are IN the denominator.

| set | items | decided | top-1 acc (decided) | abstention rate | FALSE-PRICE RATE | n wrong |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IN THE GALLERY | 84 | 77 | 100.0% | 8.3% | **0.0%** | 0 |
| refused by the collision guard | 6 | 4 | 0.0% | 33.3% | **66.7%** | 4 |
| never taught (intruders) | 18 | 0 | 0.0% | 100.0% | **0.0%** | 0 |
| open set = refused + never taught | 24 | 4 | 0.0% | 83.3% | **16.7%** | 4 |
| everything evaluated | 108 | 81 | 95.1% | 25.0% | **3.7%** | 4 |

Gates: **theta=0.1, phi=0.9, tau_mm=4.0** — `gawaah.identity`'s shipped defaults, unmodified. See [Gate provenance](#gate-provenance).

A **false price** is any confident answer that is not the truth. That includes naming a packet that is not in the gallery: an untaught item billed as Parle-G is exactly the failure invariant 7 exists to prevent, so it is counted as an error and not excluded as out of scope.

The buckets are decided by what is IN THE GALLERY, not by what the product set intended. A product the collision guard refused was never taught, however hard the shopkeeper tried, and scoring it as taught would blame recognition for an enrolment refusal.

Items the SEGMENTER refused to measure are in the denominator too, as abstentions: 1 of 108 (MERGED_CONTOUR). The counter cannot price what it cannot measure, so that is amber for a real shopper, and dropping those rows would improve every rate on this page for no reason but that they were inconvenient.

### How much of that the tape measure gave away

`identity.py` filters by FOOTPRINT before appearance is consulted. If that filter routinely left a shortlist of one, the descriptor would never have been asked a question and the accuracy above would be the mat's rather than the embedder's. Measured over 107 queries against a gallery of 14: median shortlist **4**, mean 3.66, max 4; the footprint gate answered alone (shortlist of exactly 1) on **11.2%** of them (12 queries). Of 27 abstentions in the run, 0 were `no_candidate_in_footprint` — so the footprint gate did the SHORTLISTING and appearance did the DECIDING.

## Can this instrument read a zero?

A bench that cannot detect a broken recogniser is not evidence of a working one. So the whole loop is re-run on the SAME crops with a deliberately blind descriptor — a constant vector, so every packet in the world embeds identically — and what the bench then reports is printed here.

|  | real descriptor | BLIND descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| products enrolled | 14 | 5 |
| enrolments refused by the guard | 1 | 10 |
| top-1 accuracy on the gallery (decided) | 100.0% | 100.0% |
| abstention on the gallery | 8.3% | 0.0% |
| FALSE-PRICE RATE, everything | 3.7% | 71.3% |
| median shortlist after the footprint filter | 4.0 | 1.0 |
| queries answered by footprint alone | 11.2% | 100.0% |
| ROC AUC, same vs different | 0.99644 | 0.5 |

**Read the third row twice.** A descriptor that cannot see anything at all still scores 100.0% top-1 accuracy on its gallery, with 0.0% abstentions. That is not a bug in the scoring, it is the system behaving correctly: the collision guard refuses every colliding enrolment, which thins the gallery from 14 products to 5; the footprint filter then leaves a shortlist of exactly one for 100.0% of queries, and naming the only candidate is trivially right.

**This is the argument for reporting more than one number.** "Top-1 accuracy" on its own is satisfied by a recogniser with no eyes. What catches the blind descriptor is the count of refused enrolments, the shortlist size, the AUC, and above all the false-price rate over EVERYTHING — which goes from 3.7% to 71.3%.

**Nothing in the grid strictly beats the shipped default**: every setting with fewer false prices pays for them with more abstentions on real stock. theta=0.1, phi=0.9 sits OFF the Pareto front of (false prices, gallery abstention).

The trade curve — nothing in the grid gets both fewer false prices and fewer gallery abstentions than these:

| theta | phi | false prices | gallery abstention |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.00 | 0.55 | 18 | 1.2% |
| 0.10 | 0.55 | 17 | 2.4% |
| 0.00 | 0.80 | 13 | 3.6% |
| 0.20 | 0.40 | 8 | 4.8% |
| 0.00 | 0.90 | 4 | 7.1% |
| 0.40 | 0.40 | 0 | 57.1% |

**Both gates are real levers, and they are not interchangeable.** Holding theta at its default and moving phi across the grid moves the false-price count from 22 down to 4, at a gallery abstention cost of 2.4% -> 27.4%. Holding phi at its default and moving theta moves it from 4 down to 0, costing 7.1% -> 59.5%. phi only starts to bite ABOVE 0.8, because the impostors here score 0.7-0.9 — far above any phi anyone would ship — and the worst genuine same-product cosine is 0.6697, so a phi high enough to reject the impostors is within touching distance of rejecting real views of real stock. **This bench changed nothing.** A threshold fitted to eighteen rendered rectangles is exactly the overfit `gawaah/embedder.py` documents itself having made once already, and this file does not own `gawaah/identity.py`. The numbers are here so the next person can decide with evidence instead of taste.

## Where this loses

**1. Packets that are not in the gallery.** This is the failure that costs money and it is present here: 4 of 24 open-set crops (16.7%) were confidently named and priced. Worst cases: jeera_glucose -> parle_glucose (top1 0.999, margin 0.349). The gates ARE a lever and the sweep prices it: the shipped theta=0.1/phi=0.9 costs 4 false prices at 8.3% abstention on real stock; driving it to 0 needs theta=0.40/phi=0.40 and 57.1% abstention, which is a different product. But a gallery cannot know what it has never been shown, and on a real shelf the open set is everything the shopkeeper has not got round to teaching yet — which on day one is the whole shop.

**2. Products that differ only by a half-turn.** `parle_glucose` and `jeera_glucose` are the same packet with the print at the other end. Placement reports angle in [0,180), and the descriptor folds 180-degree partners on purpose, so the two are the same picture: enrolment cosine 0.9998. The guard refused the second enrolment (yes), which is the only safe answer, but it means a real shopkeeper with two flavours in identical wrappers cannot teach both. He is told at enrolment, not at the till.

**3. Pose and light — the margin thins before the answer breaks.** No view lost a gallery product to a WRONG name, so the damage shows up as abstentions and as a shrinking margin rather than as errors. Most abstentions: `V5` (rotated 90 deg, crop 3% loose) at 28.6%. Weakest same-product score: `V4` (rotated -14 deg, bright 1.22x) bottoming out at top1 0.9123 against phi=0.9 — 0.012 of headroom left. Narrowest decision: `V5` at margin 0.1592 against theta=0.1. The pipeline de-rotates through minAreaRect and the descriptor folds the half-turn away, so what is left is interpolation loss and the crop error — and a 3% measurement error is already a 2.9 mm move against a 4.0 mm footprint gate, i.e. the next size of crop error stops being an appearance problem and becomes a no_candidate_in_footprint abstention.

**4. The live loop throws the colour away, and it costs a whole product.** `Brain._crop` (`gawaah/brain.py`) greyscales the rectified buffer before the embedder sees it; the enrol desk's `oriented_crop_bgr` keeps the colour. The clearest consequence is not a rate, it is a refusal: in grey the collision guard rejects 2 enrolment(s) (jeera_glucose, medimix_green) against 1 in colour, so `medimix_green` cannot be taught at all — it is the same size and the same layout as a packet already in the gallery and differs only in hue, which in grey is not a difference. On what survives: accuracy on decided items 100.0% -> 100.0%, abstention 8.3% -> 17.9%, and the descriptor's own separation falls (ROC AUC 0.99644 -> 0.98827, different-product pairs above the worst same-product pair 4.8% -> 17.4%). Whoever owns `gawaah/brain.py` should hand the embedder the BGR crop; this file does not own it and did not change it.

**5. The tape measure carries part of this, and the share is measured.** tau_mm = 4.0 shortlists by size BEFORE appearance is consulted. On a catalog where every packet is a different size that filter answers nearly every query on its own and the descriptor is never tested. This set fights that — the largest footprint family is 7 products at 95.0 mm (parle_glucose, jeera_glucose, krack_jack, monaco_salted, hide_seek, intruder_masala, intruder_lookalike) — and the measurement says it worked: median shortlist 4.0 of 14 gallery entries, with only 11.2% of queries answered by size alone. But that number is a property of THIS catalog: a shop whose sizes happen to be distinct will read far better here than its descriptor deserves, and a shop with twenty 38 mm sachets will read far worse.

**6. Scale, and what is untested.** 18 products, one enrolment view each, 107 evaluated crops. The gallery is scanned linearly per identify (measured 2.624 ms median, dominated by the embed) — fine at 24 SKUs, untested at thousands. Multi-view enrolment is untested: `Gallery.score` is best-of over views, so a second photograph should help and nothing here proves by how much. And every packet is rendered, so none of this is evidence about a real shelf under a real lamp.

## What is real here and what is not

**Real:** the ArUco mat lock, the 840x1188 metric buffer at 2.83 px/mm, the millimetre measurement, `gawaah.embedder`'s classical descriptor, `gawaah.identity`'s footprint-then-appearance gates at their shipped values, `gawaah.shop_store` written to and reopened from disk, and `gawaah.recogniser`'s pricing. Zero model weights, zero network.

**Not real:** the packets. They are flat rendered rectangles with chunky colour blocks, warped through a synthetic 2% tilt with sigma=4 Gaussian sensor noise. There is no specular highlight, no shadow, no crushed corner, no crumpled foil, no printed fine text and no motion blur. Real packaging is harder in every one of those directions. **Every number above is an upper bound on real-shelf behaviour, not a prediction of it.**

Measurement fidelity in this run: worst long-edge error 1.286 mm, mean 0.514 mm against known truth; 0 unmatched placement(s) and 1 item(s) the segmenter refused to measure, across 54 scenes.

Crop provenance: `tools.upload_app.oriented_crop_bgr`.

Nothing in this bench settles money. It reads integer paise out of a catalog to check that the right price came back with the right name, and it mints nothing, signs nothing and pays nothing (invariant 2).
