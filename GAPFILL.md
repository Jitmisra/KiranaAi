# Incumbent merchant products in India + the cashierless/kirana-tech failure graveyard: what a camera-native Razorpay counter product must not rebuild, and what it can honestly claim

## KEY_FINDINGS
- AMAZON JUST WALK OUT — the exact objection you will face: ~1,000 India-based workers reviewed store video; in 2022 roughly 700 of every 1,000 transactions required human review against an internal target of fewer than 50 per 1,000, receipts arrived hours later, and JWO was pulled from large-format Amazon Fresh stores (announced April 2024). Source: The Deep Dive / Business Standard / NBC summarising Business Insider's reporting.
- AMAZON'S OWN PUBLISHED REASON is not 'the AI failed' — aboutamazon.com says: 'In larger grocery stores, where customers are making a big weekly trip and buy a greater number of items, customers so far prefer Amazon Dash Cart' because 'customers tell us a running tally of purchases is more important to them. Customers want to manage their budget during their shopping journeys, in real time.' This is a direct design instruction for a kirana product: SHOW THE RUNNING TALLY, do not do silent walk-out.
- JWO did not die, it changed business model: Amazon reports 140+ third-party Just Walk Out locations across US/UK/Australia/Canada and 18M+ items sold, plus Amazon One at 500+ Whole Foods and 150+ third-party sites. The vision tech survives only where the capture volume is bounded (stadium kiosk, airport grab-and-go), never open-aisle grocery.
- GRABANGO shut down 9 Oct 2024 after raising $93M+, with live deployments at ALDI (ALDIgo), and signed deals with Circle K, Giant Eagle and 7-Eleven. Stated reason: 'was not able to secure the funding it needed to continue providing service to its clients.' Retailers chose cheaper self-checkout kiosks instead.
- STANDARD COGNITION / STANDARD AI raised $150M at a $1B valuation, then in March 2024 abandoned autonomous checkout for a camera analytics product suite called 'Vision Analytics'; layoffs accompanied the pivot; founder-CEO Jordan Fisher moved to board chairman, COO Angie Westbrock became CEO. (Search-snippet level; direct page returned 403.)
- THE SURVIVORS ALL CONSTRAINED THE CAPTURE VOLUME. Mashgin (kiosk with a tray, items pre-trained before deployment, ~10-second checkout) raised $62.5M Series B led by NEA at a $1.5B valuation. Caper was acquired by Instacart for $350M and deployed at Kroger and ShopRite as a CART. Neither watches a whole store. A single fixed phone over one counter is the same bounded-volume bet, not the Amazon bet.
- 1K KIRANA is the sharpest Indian kirana-tech death: raised $32M (INR 250 Cr+) from Info Edge, Alpha Wave, Kae Capital; peaked at 1,400 franchise stores across 25 districts in 2022; FY23 revenue INR 15.5 Cr against opex INR 17.2 Cr; workforce went from 1,000+ in Aug 2023 to ~30 by May 2024; took a bridge round in Aug 2023 at a $45M valuation, a ~60% markdown from $110M in 2022; supply chain halted late May 2024. (Inc42)
- THE CATALOG GRAVEYARD IS THE THESIS-CONFIRMING FACT: Khatabook shut MyStore in November 2021; OkCredit shut OkShop in April 2022; Dukaan, Aarzoo, Bikayi and Peel-Works pivoted or scaled down; DealShare shut its B2B vertical. Every one of these asked the kirana to TYPE IN a catalog. Not one of them tried to observe it. That is the exact hole the camera thesis fills.
- OKCREDIT UNIT ECONOMICS ARE BRUTAL AND PUBLIC: FY21 spend of INR 114.6 crore against INR 3.79 LAKH in sales revenue. Raised $67M Series B (Tiger Global, Lightspeed) after a $15.5M Series A. Laid off 30-35% of staff in Feb 2022 (140+ down to ~80). Claimed 1.7M active merchants against 6.5M registered — roughly 26% activation.
- KHATABOOK: $100M Series C at a $600M valuation (Tribe Capital, Moore Strategic Ventures, Tencent, Unilever Ventures); claims 10M+ monthly active merchants; revenue INR 52 Cr (FY22) to INR 80 Cr (FY23) to INR 105 Cr (FY24); laid off 42 people, 6% of a 700-person workforce. That is roughly INR 100 of annual revenue per claimed active merchant. THE UDHAAR-LEDGER CATEGORY WORKS AS A HABIT AND FAILS AS A BUSINESS — monetise on payment TPV, never on SaaS.
- SOUNDBOX PRICING AND SCALE (Rest of World, 2023): Paytm Soundbox INR 300 setup + INR 125/month, later cut to INR 78/month; PhonePe smart speaker INR 299 setup + INR 50-100/month varying by city; Paytm had 6.8M devices deployed and was shipping 900,000-1,000,000 per quarter; PhonePe had 2.2M. Current scale: PhonePe reported 9.19M net deployed payment devices as of 30 Sep 2025; Paytm's subscription merchant base is ~1.5 crore. Paytm soundboxes ship in 11 regional languages with up to 30-day battery and 4G.
- THE SOUNDBOX EXISTS BECAUSE OF THE FAKE-SCREENSHOT PROBLEM — a merchant quoted in Rest of World: 'people were using apps to create fake payment receipts. I got conned a few times.' The entire multi-million-device soundbox industry is a hardware workaround for a verification problem. A camera solves the same problem in software with no INR 78/month rental.
- RAZORPAY'S OWN OFFERINGS LEAVE THE KIRANA UNSERVED: the Razorpay POS promotion is explicitly for 'merchants availing 50+ devices post Aug 1, 2025' — i.e. chains, not single shops. The Bharat Soundbox (announced ~29 Aug 2024, made-in-India, LED strip showing amount and status) publishes NO price, NO rental, NO install base and NO language list on either the launch blog or razorpay.com/docs/pos/soundbox. Razorpay has hardware for chains and a soundbox with no published economics; it has nothing that gives a single kirana an INPUT device.
- HARDWARE PRICE WALL, HISTORICALLY FATAL: SnapBizz's cloud POS was INR 50,000 one-time; Nukkad Shops was INR 30,000-55,000; Reliance Jio's MPoS was INR 3,000 one-time and targeted 5 million kiranas by 2023 — against a baseline where only ~15,000 Indian retail stores were digitised as of May 2019. SnapBizz raised $12-14M (Jungle Ventures, Blume, Nielsen, Qualcomm, AUO, Ratan Tata) and reached 10,000+ stores in 7 cities against a stated plan of 150,000 stores in 42 cities. Every hardware-first kirana play undershot its target by an order of magnitude.
- QR STICKER SWAP IS A REAL, PROSECUTED, GROWING CRIME: a Pune sweet shop lost customer payments of INR 14,000 over 3 days (Jan 2026); Delhi Police arrested a 19-year-old who contacted shopkeepers on WhatsApp posing as a customer, requested their QR image, and returned a DIGITALLY EDITED code that kept the merchant's name visible while silently swapping the underlying account; in Khajuraho, MP, fraudsters swapped QR stickers overnight and hit 10 shopkeepers. The Delhi attack is invisible to the human eye and to every published piece of advice ('check if a sticker is pasted over the original').
- THE CLOSEST EXISTING COMPETITOR ON SCREENSHOT VERIFICATION IS FORENSICS-ONLY: ScamShield AI (Ahmedabad; ScamShield AI Private Limited) checks a UPI screenshot in under 3 seconds using Error Level Analysis for edited pixels, OCR of amount/VPA/date/reference, VPA reputation heuristics and QR decoding — with NO lookup against the merchant's actual bank or gateway ledger. It offers a 30-day trial and an 'INR 1 verification, auto-refunded' and publishes no accuracy figure. Forensics returns a probability; a UTR lookup against the merchant's own Razorpay ledger returns a PROOF.
- CASHFREE (a direct Razorpay competitor) markets RiskShield with 'real-time payment confirmations' and AI fraud detection, and cites one customer, 3Lines, with disputes down 80%, dispute-handling time down 90%, and INR 2 crore+ of risky payments blocked. But Cashfree's own advice to merchants is still manual: 'Never rely on customer screenshots alone; open your UPI app or check your bank SMS to confirm money arrived.' The verification step is still a human opening an app.
- CAMERA INPUT DOES EXIST IN INDIAN MERCHANT APPS — BUT ONLY ON THE PURCHASE SIDE. myBillBook lets merchants 'click photos directly from the mobile app' to OCR SUPPLIER invoices into purchase entries with live stock updates, included in paid plans from INR 291/month billed annually. Vyapar's OCR is upload-only (JPEG/PNG/PDF), one page per entry, premium-tier only, English works best with regional languages 'may come later', explicitly does not claim handwriting support, and requires an internet connection because processing is server-side. Both digitise the bill the merchant RECEIVES. Neither watches the sale the merchant MAKES.
- PRICING OF THE PAID INCUMBENTS (Techjockey, 2026): Vyapar Mobile Silver INR 699/yr, Mobile Gold INR 799/yr, Desktop Silver INR 3,799/yr, Mobile+Desktop Gold INR 4,799/yr; myBillBook Diamond INR 3,490/yr (INR 291/mo annual) and Platinum INR 3,990/yr. Vyapar claims 1.5 crore+ businesses and 17M Android downloads. Khatabook and OkCredit are free, Khatabook monetising via a ~1% payment processing fee.
- BHARATPE FY25: operating revenue INR 1,667 Cr (+16.9% from INR 1,426.1 Cr), net loss INR 88.2 Cr (down 82% from INR 492 Cr), adjusted PBT +INR 6 Cr excluding INR 148.5 Cr of ESOP cost; advertising and marketing slashed 88% to INR 26.2 Cr; service fee income (including device and soundbox rental) was 77.6% of operating revenue. Company claims 17M+ merchant partners, INR 1,700+ Bn TPV, INR 14,600+ Cr of loans facilitated across 450+ cities; ~0.8M soundboxes deployed in FY23. THE MERCHANT-DEVICE BUSINESS ONLY REACHED BREAKEVEN BY CUTTING MARKETING 88% — distribution to kiranas is the expensive part, not the tech.
- HANDWRITTEN DEVANAGARI OCR IS THE WEAKEST LINK IN THE PLAN: published claims put printed Hindi Devanagari OCR at ~95% but CLEAR handwritten Hindi at only 60-75% (FastOCR's own vendor claim), with conjunct characters and matras cited as the structural problem and limited benchmark datasets. Any udhaar-ledger-to-payment-link flow must be merchant-confirmed per row, never auto-minted.
- B2B KIRANA SUPPLY CONSOLIDATED RATHER THAN SUCCEEDED: Udaan has raised $1B+ total, took another $114M at a $1.8B valuation and is still not at breakeven; Jumbotail raised $120M led by Standard Chartered Ventures, hit unicorn status and is merging with Standard Chartered's Solv; ShopKirana (Info Edge-backed) is being absorbed by Udaan. Nobody in this category is monetising the kirana's own counter — they monetise the wholesale order.

## WHITE_SPACE
- NOBODY IN INDIA POINTS A CAMERA AT THE SELL SIDE. Every camera feature in every Indian merchant app (myBillBook photo-capture OCR, Vyapar OCR) digitises the SUPPLIER INVOICE the merchant receives. Zero products observe what leaves the shelf and turn it into a payable amount. This is the single cleanest piece of white space found.
- CATALOG-BY-OBSERVATION IS UNCLAIMED AND THE GRAVEYARD PROVES THE DEMAND. Khatabook's MyStore (dead Nov 2021), OkCredit's OkShop (dead Apr 2022), Dukaan and Bikayi all asked the kirana to TYPE a catalog and all died or pivoted. Not one competitor, dead or alive, ever tried to BUILD the catalog by watching. The strategic thesis in the brief SURVIVES this research — and the graveyard is evidence FOR it, not against it: the reason agentic commerce cannot reach kiranas is precisely that catalog entry is the step merchants will not do, and observation is the only known way around a step merchants will not do.
- QR TAMPER DETECTION AT THE MODULE-MATRIX LEVEL IS UNCLAIMED. Every published defence in India is a human eyeball instruction ('check whether a sticker looks pasted over the original'). The documented Delhi Police modus operandi — a digitally edited QR that keeps the merchant's NAME visible while swapping the underlying account — defeats eyeballs completely. Decoding the payee VPA plus XOR-ing the module matrix against a registered baseline is a defence nobody ships. ScamShield AI 'decodes and flags suspicious QR codes' but has no registered-baseline comparison.
- SCREENSHOT VERIFICATION AGAINST THE MERCHANT'S OWN SETTLED LEDGER IS UNCLAIMED. ScamShield AI does image forensics (ELA + OCR + VPA heuristics) and returns a probability. Cashfree's own recommended workflow is 'open your UPI app and check' — a manual human step. An OCR of the UTR/RRN followed by an exact lookup in the merchant's own Razorpay payments list is categorically stronger: it cannot be beaten by a better forger, because a perfect forgery of a payment that never happened still returns NOT FOUND.
- HANDWRITTEN UDHAAR LEDGER TO PAYMENT LINK IS UNCLAIMED. Khatabook and OkCredit built the entire udhaar category on MANUAL RE-TYPING of the paper book. Neither reads the existing paper book. The migration cost of the physical bahi khata is the reason activation stalls (OkCredit: 1.7M active on 6.5M registered), and no one has attacked it with a camera.
- THE SOUNDBOX IS A ONE-BIT OUTPUT DEVICE OCCUPYING EXACTLY THE POSITION YOU WANT. 9.19M PhonePe devices and ~1M/quarter of Paytm devices sit on Indian counters emitting 'amount received' and taking in NOTHING. A propped phone occupies the same physical slot and is an INPUT device. Nobody has framed the counter camera as 'the soundbox, but it can see' — and it costs INR 0/month against INR 50-125/month of rental.
- RAZORPAY-SPECIFIC OPENING: Razorpay POS is explicitly gated at 50+ devices (chains). Bharat Soundbox publishes no price and no install base. Razorpay has NO published product that makes a single, computer-less kirana transactable. A phone-only, zero-hardware path into the same merchant is not competitive with any Razorpay product line — it is the missing bottom of their funnel, which is the most fundable framing on a hackathon stage.
- BLUNT ASSESSMENT — THE HONEST CASE THAT A CAMERA-NATIVE PRODUCT SUCCEEDS WHERE THESE DID NOT, in four claims that survive the graveyard: (1) BOUNDED CAPTURE VOLUME. Amazon's 700-in-1000 human-review rate came from tracking many anonymous shoppers through open aisles with multi-person occlusion and shelf-return ambiguity. Mashgin (tray) and Caper (cart) are the two CV checkout businesses still standing and BOTH survived by shrinking the capture volume to one bounded surface. One fixed phone over one counter, one hand, one item stream, ~200 SKUs is the Mashgin geometry, not the Amazon geometry. (2) HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP IS A FEATURE, NOT A SECRET. Amazon's scandal was that the reviewer was HIDDEN and OFFSHORE. Here the reviewer is the shopkeeper, present, and confirms with a gesture before the QR is minted. Say this line on stage before a judge says it for you. (3) ZERO HARDWARE. Every kirana hardware play — SnapBizz at INR 50,000, Nukkad at INR 30-55,000, Jio at INR 3,000 chasing 5M stores — undershot by an order of magnitude, and 1K Kirana burned $32M into a INR 15.5 Cr revenue line. A product that ships as software onto a phone the merchant already owns removes the exact variable that killed all of them. (4) MONETISE ON TPV, NOT SaaS. Khatabook's INR 105 Cr on 10M claimed active merchants (~INR 100/merchant/year) and OkCredit's INR 114.6 Cr spent against INR 3.79 lakh earned are the two hardest numbers in this brief: Indian micro-merchants will use a free habit forever and pay for software almost never. The only durable revenue at this counter is a cut of a payment that would not otherwise have been digital. THE HONEST WEAKNESS: none of this has been demonstrated at kirana lighting, kirana SKU density, or kirana network quality by anyone, and the fact that no incumbent tried it is weak evidence that it is hard as well as strong evidence that it is open. The winning posture is therefore ASSISTIVE AND MEASURED — publish precision/recall on a held-out set for the QR-tamper and screenshot verifiers (Track 02), publish a match rate and an honest exception list on 50+ records (Track 04), and let the observed-catalog-to-agentic-buyer demo (Track 01) be the vision on top of two things that are numerically proven.

## FEATURE_IDEAS
- TWO-SIDED PHONE: run a live running tally on the screen facing the customer while the camera watches the counter. This is a direct quote-backed answer to Amazon's OWN stated reason for retreating from Just Walk Out ('customers tell us a running tally of purchases is more important to them... in real time'). It converts the graveyard's biggest story into a design credential.
- SOFTWARE SOUNDBOX: after the qr_code.credited webhook fires, speak the confirmation in the merchant's language from the same phone. You reproduce the single function that 9.19M PhonePe devices and ~1M/quarter of Paytm devices exist to perform, at INR 0/month against INR 50-125/month of rental — and you add an input channel the soundbox does not have.
- MERCHANT-IN-THE-LOOP AS THE HEADLINE, NOT THE FOOTNOTE: a MediaPipe gesture confirm before the QR is minted. Script the stage line: 'Amazon hid a thousand reviewers in India and called it AI. We put one reviewer in the loop, on purpose, and he is the shopkeeper.' This pre-empts the only objection a retail-savvy judge will raise.
- CATALOG-BY-OBSERVATION EXPORT (Track 01 wedge): after N days of watching, emit a machine-readable catalog — SKU, observed price, observed daily velocity, confidence — that an AI buyer or ONDC-style agent can transact against. Frame it explicitly as the thing MyStore (dead Nov 2021), OkShop (dead Apr 2022) and Dukaan all died trying to make merchants type.
- QR TAMPER: register the module matrix AND the decoded payee VPA at onboarding, then re-verify daily from the counter camera. Show the Delhi Police case on stage — merchant name intact, account silently swapped — as the attack that a human eye and every published Indian safety guide provably miss, and that a matrix XOR catches deterministically.
- SCREENSHOT VERIFY WITH A TERNARY VERDICT, NOT A SCORE: OCR the UTR/RRN, then exact-lookup against the merchant's own Razorpay payments list, and return SETTLED / NOT FOUND / FOUND BUT AMOUNT MISMATCH. Position explicitly against ScamShield AI's ELA-based forensics: 'forensics returns a probability that can be beaten by a better forger; a ledger lookup returns a proof that cannot, because a perfect image of a payment that never happened is still NOT FOUND.'
- TRACK 04 RECONCILIATION LOOP: match observed-sale events against qr_code.credited webhooks over 50+ records and publish match rate plus an honest exception list (item seen but never paid; paid but nothing seen; amount mismatch). The exception list IS the product for a shopkeeper — it is the first time shrinkage and payment leakage at a kirana counter have ever been quantified.
- PAPER-BOOK IMPORT AS THE ONBOARDING WEDGE: photograph the existing bahi khata page and mint Razorpay Payment Links per row, with a mandatory per-row merchant confirm (justified by the 60-75% handwritten Devanagari OCR ceiling). This attacks the exact re-typing cost that left OkCredit with 1.7M active merchants on a 6.5M registered base.
- PRICING POSTURE: never sell SaaS. Khatabook earns ~INR 100/merchant/year and OkCredit spent INR 114.6 Cr to earn INR 3.79 lakh. Take a cut of TPV that would not otherwise have been digital, exactly as Khatabook's ~1% processing fee does, and say so in the business slide.

## RISKS
- THE AMAZON OBJECTION IS THE ONE THAT KILLS YOU IF UNPREPARED: 700 of 1,000 transactions needed a human. If a judge raises it and you have no answer, the demo reads as a re-run. The answer must be geometric (one counter, one fixed view, one hand, bounded SKU set = Mashgin's tray, not Amazon's aisle) AND architectural (merchant confirms before the QR mints). Have it rehearsed in one breath.
- AMAZON'S RETREAT WAS ALSO A CUSTOMER-PREFERENCE FINDING, NOT ONLY A COST ONE — shoppers wanted a visible running tally. A silent 'we saw it, here's a QR' UX repeats the exact thing Amazon abandoned. If your demo has no live tally facing the customer, you have rebuilt the failed design.
- HANDWRITTEN DEVANAGARI OCR AT 60-75% IS NOT PRODUCTION-SAFE. Auto-minting Payment Links from a misread udhaar row creates a real financial error in front of judges. Gate every row behind a confirm and state the accuracy honestly rather than claiming a number you cannot defend.
- THE LEDGER CATEGORY'S MONETISATION IS A PUBLIC EMBARRASSMENT AND JUDGES MAY KNOW IT: OkCredit spent INR 114.6 Cr to earn INR 3.79 lakh in FY21; Khatabook earns ~INR 105 Cr across 10M claimed active merchants. Any slide implying merchants will pay a subscription will be shot down. Revenue must be framed as a share of newly-digital TPV.
- RETENTION, NOT ACQUISITION, IS THE KIRANA KILLER: OkCredit's ~26% active-to-registered ratio is the category benchmark. A camera product that requires the merchant to prop the phone correctly every morning has a daily ritual cost higher than opening an app. Address propping/setup explicitly or the retention objection lands.
- DISTRIBUTION IS THE EXPENSIVE PART AND EVERY PREDECESSOR PROVED IT: BharatPe only reached breakeven by cutting marketing 88% to INR 26.2 Cr; 1K Kirana burned $32M to reach INR 15.5 Cr of revenue; SnapBizz reached 10,000 of a planned 150,000 stores; Jio targeted 5M kiranas from a base of ~15,000 digitised stores. Do not present a go-to-market that assumes kiranas will find you.
- CV CHECKOUT AS AN ENTERPRISE HARDWARE SALE IS A DEAD BUSINESS MODEL — Grabango ($93M, ALDI/Circle K/7-Eleven deals) still could not raise, and Standard Cognition ($150M, $1B valuation) walked away to sell analytics instead. If your pitch smells like 'sell cameras to stores', you inherit their fundraising story. Phone-only is not a compromise, it is the differentiator.
- COMPETITIVE OVERLAP EXISTS AND MUST BE ACKNOWLEDGED, NOT HIDDEN: myBillBook already does camera-capture OCR of supplier invoices, and ScamShield AI already sells fake-screenshot checking. Claiming 'nobody does camera' is factually wrong and a judge may know it. Claim precisely: nobody points the camera at the SELL side, nobody verifies against the merchant's OWN ledger, nobody compares a QR against a REGISTERED baseline.
- PRIVACY AND CONSENT: a phone filming customers at an Indian counter, including faces and hands, is a real objection. Have an answer — on-device inference, no face storage, item-region crops only, merchant-owned data — before it is asked.
- SCOPE RISK IN 8 DAYS: six capabilities across three tracks is more surface than one solo builder can make numerically defensible. The graveyard research says the two capabilities with hard, measurable, held-out numbers (QR tamper detection, screenshot-vs-ledger verification) are the ones that survive scrutiny; the catalog-by-observation demo is the vision layer. Weight build time accordingly.
- SEVERAL PUBLISHED FRAUD STATISTICS CIRCULATING ON INDIAN BLOGS COULD NOT BE TRACED TO A PRIMARY SOURCE — do not put an unsourced 'INR X crore of UPI fraud' number on a slide. Use the named police cases (Pune INR 14,000; Delhi 19-year-old arrest; Khajuraho 10 shopkeepers) and the merchant quote from Rest of World, which are attributable.

# Making a shop with no computer transactable by an AI buyer — the Track 01 catalog bridge (Razorpay AI Buildathon 2026)

## KEY_FINDINGS
- THESIS SURVIVES, WITH EVIDENCE: Google's own UCP merchant onboarding page (developers.google.com/merchant/ucp) instructs merchants to 'use your existing Merchant Center account shopping feeds' — i.e. the entry ticket to Google's agentic surface IS a pre-existing product feed. ACP's product feed spec makes `url` (product detail page, RFC 1738, must resolve HTTP 200) a REQUIRED field. Shopify's agent catalog presupposes a Shopify store. Every agentic-commerce on-ramp in 2026 is a re-skin of an existing e-commerce catalog. There is literally no defined path for a merchant whose catalog exists only in his head. The gate is real and it is the catalog.
- ACP PRODUCT FEED — REQUIRED FIELDS (developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/feed): item_id (alphanumeric, max 100), title (UTF-8, max 150), description (max 5,000, plain text), brand (max 70), url (must return HTTP 200), image_url, price (number + ISO 4217), availability (enum: in_stock | out_of_stock | pre_order | backorder | unknown), seller_name (max 70), is_eligible_search (bool), is_eligible_checkout (bool, requires is_eligible_search=true plus seller_privacy_policy and seller_tos URLs), target_countries (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 — CURRENTLY SUPPORTS US ONLY). Recommended: gtin (8–14 digits), mpn, group_id, listing_has_variations, variant_dict, size (max 20), size_system. identifier_exists governs whether gtin/mpn is mandatory. Formats: UTF-8 .txt/.tsv/.csv or gzipped; refresh as often as every 15 minutes.
- GOOGLE MERCHANT CENTER REQUIRED: id, title (or structured_title), description (or structured_description), link, image_link, availability, price. Conditionally required: brand (all new products except movies/books/musical recordings), gtin (strongly recommended; mpn required only if no manufacturer GTIN; else identifier_exists=no), condition (required if used/refurbished), availability_date (required if preorder). Recommended: color, size, gender, age_group, item_group_id, product_type, material. (support.google.com/merchants/answer/7052112)
- THE UNLOCK NOBODY IS USING — GOOGLE LOCAL PRODUCT INVENTORY FEED: required attributes are only store_code, id, availability, and pickup_sla (required for items pickable in-store). quantity, price, pickup_method and sale_price are OPTIONAL (support.google.com/merchants/answer/3061342). And India IS a supported country for BOTH local inventory ads and free local listings (support.google.com/merchants/answer/3057972). A camera that watches a shelf produces `availability` continuously — which is the single hardest field for any static feed to keep true and the one field the local feed absolutely demands. This is a real, India-live destination for camera-derived data.
- SCHEMA.ORG / GOOGLE MERCHANT LISTING: Product REQUIRED = name, image, offers. Offer REQUIRED = price (or priceSpecification.price) and priceCurrency (ISO 4217, 3-letter). Everything else is RECOMMENDED: availability, url, priceValidUntil, shippingDetails, hasMerchantReturnPolicy, itemCondition, validFrom, brand.name, gtin/gtin8/12/13/14, mpn, sku, aggregateRating, review. Merchant listings must use `Offer`, not `AggregateOffer` (the merchant must be the seller). The required surface is astonishingly small — three Product fields and two Offer fields.
- UCP IS REAL AND IS THE BIGGEST THING IN THE SPACE: Universal Commerce Protocol, ucp.dev, GitHub Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp, Apache 2.0, 3.3k stars / 451 forks. Announced by Sundar Pichai at the NRF 2026 keynote on 11 January 2026. Co-developed by Google and Shopify; ucp.dev names Google, Amazon, Shopify, Walmart, Stripe, Marriott, DoorDash and 50+ organisations. Discovery is a manifest at /.well-known/ucp containing {ucp: {version, services, capabilities, payment_handlers}, keys: [JWK Set for signatures]}. Capability IDs: dev.ucp.shopping.checkout, .cart, .catalog, .order, .permalink; dev.ucp.common.identity_linking, .location. Extensions: .fulfillment, .discount, .buyer_consent, common.extensions.loyalty; payment extensions ap2_mandates, authentication, terms, split_payments. Checkout endpoints POST /checkout-sessions and PUT /checkout-sessions/{id}. Transport-agnostic across REST, MCP and A2A.
- UCP's manifest already carries a `keys` array (a JWK Set for signatures) — meaning the protocol anticipates cryptographically signed merchant responses. This is the exact hook a signed/attested catalog would hang on, and it is unoccupied.
- SHOPIFY CATALOG FOR AGENTS conforms to the UCP Catalog capability. Global Catalog at https://catalog.shopify.com/api/ucp/mcp; Storefront Catalog at https://{storeDomain}/api/ucp/mcp. Three tools: search_catalog (keyword; global results clustered by UPID = Universal Product ID across merchants), lookup_catalog (batch by identifier — up to 50 ids global, 10 storefront), get_product (full detail with variant availability and checkout links). No API key; every request must carry an agent profile URL at meta.ucp-agent.profile. Shopify explicitly warns that 'some fields are inferred by AI and may have varying accuracy' — a precedent for shipping a probabilistic catalog.
- ACP AGENTIC CHECKOUT — merchant must implement POST /checkout_sessions, POST /checkout_sessions/{id}, POST /checkout_sessions/{id}/complete, POST /checkout_sessions/{id}/cancel, GET /checkout_sessions/{id}. Required headers on every call: Authorization, Accept-Language, Idempotency-Key, Request-Id, Signature (base64 body signature), Timestamp (RFC 3339), API-Version. Session status enum: not_ready_for_payment | ready_for_payment | completed | canceled. totals must itemise base, discount, subtotal, tax, fulfillment, fee, total. Merchant emits HMAC-signed webhooks back: order_created and order_updated, with order status created | manual_review | confirmed | canceled | shipped | fulfilled.
- ACP DELEGATED PAYMENT: POST /agentic_commerce/delegate_payment (OpenAI → PSP). PSP returns a single-use vault token prefixed `vt_`. Scoped by an allowance object: reason (currently only 'one_time'), max_amount, currency, checkout_session_id, merchant_id, expires_at (RFC 3339). Request also carries payment_method (network token preferred over FPAN), risk_signals (type/score/action) and metadata. Merchant receives the vault token at the complete-checkout call and charges through its own PSP — merchant stays merchant of record, OpenAI is NOT MoR. Stripe's Shared Payment Token is the first compatible implementation. Note the shape is card-vault-centric; India's rails are UPI, so this is where an Indian bridge has to be improvised.
- AP2 (ap2-protocol.org, Google, announced 16 Sept 2025 with 60+ partners incl. Mastercard, PayPal, Coinbase, Amex, Salesforce) defines three signed Mandates carried as W3C Verifiable Credentials: Intent Mandate (scope/rules of the task), Cart Mandate (locks exact items, quantities, price, terms), Payment Mandate (authorises against an instrument, shared with credential provider, networks, processor). UCP integrates AP2 via the dev.ucp.payment.extensions.ap2_mandates extension. The Cart Mandate is the buyer-side signed counterpart to what a signed catalog would be on the merchant side.
- NPCI UNIFIED AGENT PROTOCOL (UAP) is real but IN DEVELOPMENT, not GA. Reported July 2026 (Business Standard, Outlook Business, Medianama): a verification/registry layer on top of UPI where AI agents are registered, verified and authorised to transact; built on the existing UPI Circle delegation feature; one-time consent-based authentication with per-merchant spending limits so the agent transacts without PIN/OTP each time. It does not change the underlying UPI rails. Do not claim a live UAP integration.

## WHITE_SPACE
- NOBODY SIGNS STOCK. GS1 VCs sign product identity; AP2 Cart Mandates sign a cart at purchase time; ACP signs request bodies; UCP publishes a JWK Set. No spec anywhere defines an attestation that says 'at time T this shelf held N units of GTIN X, and here is the evidence.' A hash-chained availability attestation — each depletion event appended, each carrying a frame perceptual hash AND the Razorpay payment id that corroborates it, chain root published in /.well-known/ucp under the existing `keys` slot — is genuinely novel and is the one thing a camera can produce that a Shopify store structurally cannot.
- INVENTORY TRUTH BACKED BY MONEY TRUTH. Every feed on earth is a merchant's unverified self-report. Razorpay uniquely holds the settlement side. Binding each stock decrement to a settled qr_code.credited webhook makes the availability claim expensive to fake. Nobody does this because nobody else has both the camera and the ledger.
- THE MISSING `url`. ACP requires a product detail page that returns HTTP 200 and GMC requires `link`. A kirana has no website — so the on-ramp is structurally closed, and no product on the market mints a hosted PDP per SKU as a byproduct of onboarding. 'Catalog + hosted PDP + /.well-known/ucp manifest, all generated from counter footage' is unoccupied territory.
- THE LOCAL INVENTORY FEED IS AN OPEN DOOR INTO INDIA THAT ALMOST NOBODY WALKS THROUGH. Required attributes are only store_code, id, availability, pickup_sla; India supports both local inventory ads and free local listings. The single required dynamic field is exactly the field a camera generates for free.
- GTIN-LESS COMMERCE. identifier_exists=no is permitted by both GMC and ACP, but every tool in the ecosystem is built assuming a GTIN. ~48.62% of India's staple-food product format is raw/unprocessed. A catalog primitive designed for loose, weighed, unbranded goods — visual class + weight + observed price, with an 'ask the shop' agent fallback — does not exist.
- PROVENANCE-SCORED CATALOG FIELDS. Shopify already ships the disclaimer that 'some fields are inferred by AI and may have varying accuracy' but exposes no per-field confidence. Attaching provenance (barcode | OCR | VLM | shopkeeper-confirmed | observed-transaction) and a confidence score to every field, and only setting is_eligible_checkout=true above a threshold, is honest, demo-able, and unclaimed.
- PRICE FROM SETTLEMENT, NOT FROM THE PACK. Indian packs carry a legally printed MRP, but kiranas sell below MRP and price loose goods per kg. Learning the real selling price from the amounts the shopkeeper actually charges via Razorpay is a better price oracle than any OCR of the packet — and no feed spec contemplates a price derived from transactions.
- A DATA FLYWHEEL BACK INTO OPEN FOOD FACTS. India has only 22,716 products in OFF against 4.71M worldwide. A camera product that contributes verified Indian barcodes and pack images back under ODbL creates a public good that compounds and gives the pitch a story nobody else has.

## RISKS
- ACP product feed `target_countries` currently supports US only. You cannot demonstrate a real ChatGPT Instant Checkout purchase from an Indian kirana. Demo against your own agent client, and never imply a live ChatGPT integration — a judge who knows the spec will catch it.
- ACP and GMC both REQUIRE a product URL that returns HTTP 200. If you don't host generated PDPs, your feed is invalid by inspection. Treat this as a build requirement, not a nice-to-have.
- Open Food Facts India coverage is 22,716 products (~0.48% of the global DB). Do not promise a barcode hit rate on stage. Measure your actual hit rate on your own test shelf and report that number honestly.
- GS1 India DataKart — the one database with genuine Indian FMCG coverage — is login-gated with paid premium tiers and no documented public API. You cannot build on it in 8 days and should not imply you have.
- UPCitemdb's free tier is 100 requests/day with a 6/minute burst cap. It will fall over on a shelf with hundreds of SKUs and cannot be a runtime dependency. Prefetch an offline OFF dump instead.
- Google Merchant Center image policy: a photo taken by a counter camera may be rejected (framing, lighting, overlays), and the 500×500 minimum is enforced from 31 January 2027. Either crop tightly or substitute the GTIN-resolved OFF image.
- PRIVACY. A camera over a counter records customers' faces and payment behaviour. Have a concrete answer before you're asked: on-device inference, face blurring at capture, no face embeddings stored, shopkeeper-visible recording indicator. A hostile panel will ask, and 'we'll figure it out' loses the room.
- 'Isn't this just ONDC / DigiDukaan?' is the most likely killer question. DPIIT and ONDC convened on exactly catalogue standardisation for 1.4 crore kiranas in June 2026. Your differentiator must be stated crisply: they onboard via human field agents once; you observe continuously, so availability stays true and price comes from settled transactions.
- GST and Legal Metrology exposure once prices are published publicly. Most kiranas are unregistered or under composition scheme, and ACP's totals object demands an itemised tax line. Say 'test mode, no live tax representation' rather than fabricating a tax field.
- Both ACP and UCP are date-versioned and moving fast (ACP latest 2026-04-17, five releases since 2025-09-29). Pin the exact spec version in your README so your feed doesn't silently drift out of conformance between build and judging.
- Never claim a live NPCI UAP integration or an agentic-UPI production hookup. UAP is reported as in development (July 2026). Overclaiming here is the fastest way to lose a Razorpay panel that knows the internal status.
- Scope risk: the camera product has six advertised capabilities and eight days. The catalog bridge alone (detection → GTIN/OCR resolution → feed emission → hosted PDP → UCP manifest → MCP endpoint → Razorpay binding → attestation chain) is a full build. Pick this as the spine and let the others be supporting scenes.
