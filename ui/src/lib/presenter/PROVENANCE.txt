advisor.webp — the Advisor screen's presenter plate
===================================================

WHAT IT IS
    A head-and-shoulders portrait of a fictional character, used as the still
    plate that the Advisor's lip-sync rig draws a live mouth onto. It is a flat
    vector-style ILLUSTRATION. It is not a photograph, it is not a likeness of
    any real person, and it is not intended to be mistaken for one. The screen
    that shows it says so on the tile, under the tile, and in the limits card.

WHERE IT CAME FROM
    Generated once, on 2026-09-03, with xAI `grok-imagine-image-2.0` via
    POST https://api.x.ai/v1/images/generations. Three candidates were
    generated at $0.04 each ($0.12 total); this is the chosen one. No further
    API calls are made at any point — see CSP below.

    The prompt, verbatim:

      Flat vector illustration, clean cel-shaded 2D character art, bold clean
      shapes, smooth flat colour fills, soft simple shading, editorial
      app-illustration style. NOT a photograph, no photorealism, no skin
      texture, no pores, no film grain. Head-and-shoulders front-facing
      portrait of a friendly young South Asian woman who advises a small shop.
      Dark hair tied back, simple indigo kurta with a plain collar. Symmetrical,
      facing the viewer straight on, calm neutral expression, LIPS CLOSED and
      relaxed, eyes open looking at the viewer. Centred, whole head inside the
      frame with clear space above the hair, shoulders visible at the bottom
      edge. Plain flat dark navy background, one solid colour, no pattern, no
      text, no logo.

    No real person was named, described or referenced in the prompt, and no
    reference image was supplied.

WHAT WAS DONE TO IT
    1. Cropped from 912x1136 to 912x684 (4:3, the shape of the call tile),
       taking rows 44..728.
    2. The painted mouth was removed — an ellipse at (459, 487) r=(78, 40) in
       source coordinates, filled by OpenCV's Telea inpainting and feathered —
       so that the rig's own mouth is the only mouth on the face. Without this
       there would be a live mouth drawn on top of a permanent painted one.
    3. Encoded as WebP at quality 86.
    Nothing else was retouched. The eyes, brows, hair and clothing are as
    generated.

LICENCE
    Output of the xAI image API. Under the xAI Terms of Service, xAI assigns to
    the user all of its right, title and interest in output generated for that
    user, including for commercial use. The character is fictional and no
    third-party material is incorporated. No stock library, no scraped
    photograph, and no other party's asset is present in this file.

WHY THERE IS A SECOND COPY
    `ui/src/lib/presenter/advisor.webp` is byte-identical to this file. The till
    (`tools/upload_app.py`) mounts only `/assets` from the built site, so a file
    served from the public directory would 404 in production. The copy under
    `src/` is imported by `components/Presenter.tsx`, which makes Vite emit it
    into `dist/assets/` with a content hash — the one path this server actually
    serves. This file is the canonical vendored original and the record of where
    it came from.

CONTENT SECURITY POLICY
    The page is served under `connect-src 'self'` and `img-src 'self' data:
    blob:`. This image is loaded from this origin and nothing else is fetched.
    No request is made to xAI, or to any other host, at runtime.
